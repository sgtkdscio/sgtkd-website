SGTKD-SCIO aims to respect your privacy and explain clearly how this website uses cookies and similar technologies. This policy should be read alongside any privacy information we provide about personal information.

2. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by a website. Similar technologies can also store or access information on your device. Some are essential for a service you request; others may be used for analytics, preferences or advertising.

3. Cookies used by this website

The website files currently reviewed do not contain code that deliberately sets or reads cookies, uses local storage or session storage, or loads analytics or advertising scripts. The website does not currently appear to include an embedded Facebook feed or other iframe in the supplied homepage.

However, the hosting platform or services loaded by the website may use their own technologies. We have not independently verified every cookie that may be set by the hosting infrastructure or in every visitor's browser. This information will be updated if the website's use of cookies or similar technologies changes.

4. Strictly necessary technologies

If cookies or similar technologies are used because they are strictly necessary to provide a service you have requested, or to meet another applicable legal exemption, consent may not be required. Where applicable, we will provide information about their purpose. Non-essential technologies that require consent will not be activated before the required consent is obtained.

5. Google Fonts and other third-party resources

The homepage loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests these fonts, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details. This is an external resource, not by itself evidence that this website sets a cookie. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

The website also links to external services, including Facebook and the Information Commissioner's Office. Following an external link takes you to that provider's website, where its own privacy and cookie practices apply.

6. Analytics and marketing

No analytics or advertising scripts were identified in the website files supplied for review. If analytics, advertising or other non-essential technologies are introduced in the future, they will be assessed before use and consent will be requested where required by law.

7. Managing cookies

You can usually view, block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect some website features. Browser controls do not necessarily prevent all third-party data processing when an external resource is loaded.

For general information about cookies and UK data protection, visit the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
