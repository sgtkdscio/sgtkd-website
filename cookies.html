1. About this policy

SGTKD-SCIO is committed to respecting your privacy and being transparent about how our website operates. This policy explains what cookies are and how they may be used on our website.

2. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website may store on your device when you visit. They can support essential website functions, maintain security and remember preferences or measure website usage.

3. Strictly necessary cookies

Strictly necessary cookies and similar technologies may be used where they are essential to provide a service you have requested, maintain security or ensure the website operates correctly.

These technologies do not require consent where they meet the applicable legal exemption. We will provide information about their purpose where relevant.

4. Analytics and marketing cookies

SGTKD-SCIO does not intend to use cookies for advertising or behavioural tracking. Any analytics, functionality or other non-essential cookies introduced in the future will be assessed before use. Where consent is legally required, it will be obtained before those technologies are activated.

5. Third-party services

Some website features, such as external links or embedded services, may involve third-party providers. Those providers may process information or use cookies when you interact with their services. Their own privacy and cookie policies apply to their services.

6. Managing cookies

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect the operation of some websites or services.

For further information, visit the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) .

7. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or how SGTKD-SCIO handles information, please contact us through the contact details provided on our website.
