01 WHO WE ARE

MORE THAN
A SPORTS CLUB.

SGTKD-SCIO is a Scottish Charitable Incorporated Organisation focused on creating positive opportunities for people and communities.

We use accessible Taekwon-Do, recreation, volunteering, mentoring, outreach and cultural activity as practical ways to support participation, personal development and community connection.
